# Advisor Booking Copy Centering Design

## Goal

Complete the advisor panel's centered visual hierarchy by centering the supporting booking sentence.

## Scope

Refine only the alignment of `Choose a convenient time to discuss your student’s goals.` within the advisor booking panel. Preserve all other typography, spacing, controls, interactions, URLs, motion, and responsive geometry.

## Alignment

- Center the supporting sentence on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- Keep the existing font size, line height, color, and margins.
- Allow the sentence to wrap naturally at narrow widths without clipping.
- Do not alter the centered booking button, WhatsApp alternative, panel padding, panel gap, or header alignment.

## Responsive Behavior

- Apply the same centered alignment in the mobile bottom sheet and the anchored `320px` tablet/desktop popup.
- Preserve the mobile inset sheet and existing tablet/desktop popup position and dimensions.

## Accessibility and Verification

- Keep the sentence as semantic paragraph text.
- Verify light and dark states at mobile, tablet, and desktop widths.
- Confirm no changes to focus order, focus restoration, reduced motion, overflow, clipping, or page errors.

## Success Criteria

The supporting sentence aligns with the centered header, primary booking action, and WhatsApp alternative, creating one coherent vertical composition without changing its spacing or prominence.
